ABOUT

I work where UX
and engineering overlap.

I’m Yuqing, a product designer who builds. I start from the user problem, make a clear design judgment, and turn it into an interactive prototype the team can test — then partner with PMs and engineers to ship it.

HOW I WORK

01 · FRAMEFind the real problem

What is the user trying to do on this screen, and what is getting in the way?

02 · DECIDEMake the call

Compare a few directions, pick one, and write down why — and what we give up.

03 · PROTOTYPEBuild it in code

I direct AI coding tools for the first version, so real states, data, and edge cases show up early.

04 · REVIEWCritique and iterate

Walk the team through the prototype, review what the AI made, and refine before it ships.

WHAT I BRING

UX judgment. I can explain why a design is right for the user, not just how it looks.

Prototypes that behave like the product. Long translations, empty states, missing data — I test them in a working prototype instead of discovering them after launch.

AI-native workflow. I use Claude Code, Codex, and Cursor daily, and I’ve designed how coding agents read a design system through MCP.
